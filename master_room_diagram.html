What “low strobe risk” means

  • The model checks the published nominal beam against the entire 52-inch swept blade disk.
  • A published beam angle is not a hard cutoff; weaker spill light exists outside it.
  • If a blade does interrupt a beam, the three-blade fan produces approximately 1.75–10.05 blade passes per second across its published speed range.
  • Actual brightness and shadow contrast also depend on dimmer level, wall reflectance, bedding, and daylight.

Items to verify before cutting

  • Laser-check the 140″ peak and both wall heights.
  • Confirm the beam underside, exposed depth, width, and that its top face is concealed at the ridge.
  • Measure from the floor to the bottom surface of one stationary fan blade.
  • Confirm the 77″ fan-center position by projecting the hub vertically to the floor.
  • Have the electrician locate joists and confirm the required 6½-inch cutout and overhead clearance.
  • Confirm the selected dimmer with Feit’s compatibility guidance and test low-end behavior before installing all eight.

This is a geometric planning study, not a construction drawing or a substitute for an electrician’s field verification. Do not cut from the drawing alone.
