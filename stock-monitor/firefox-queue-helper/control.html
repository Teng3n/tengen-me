Behavior and limitations

A secure connection receives hosted checks: normally five seconds, with a bounded one-minute two-second burst after either source turns positive. No Telegram reading or additional local Blizzard requests. A fresh BOTH-positive snapshot can trigger immediately upon arming; tests, old checks, partial signals, and repeated events cannot.

Telegram still warns on either positive; this extension requires BOTH inventory Yes and button Enabled in the same healthy check. Opens and selects the fixed one-copy Shop Pay tab, requests Firefox foreground focus, and sounds an alarm. Stock, queue entry, price, quantity, and payment remain your responsibility. Your $250 limit is not automatically enforced. Never clicks Pay now.

Disconnected means no real-time delivery. Same-helper reconnects use backoff and preserve arming unless the computer slept or the control tab stalled. A persistent receiver identity and increasing attempt counter distinguish newer reconnects from delayed handshakes. A different receiver is rejected while this one is healthy; it cannot silently disconnect this helper. Use one normal non-container Firefox profile. Do not copy extension storage between profiles.

Checks older than 20 seconds are ignored; keep Windows clock correct. The one-attempt latch is saved before opening. Restart does not reset it or automatically arm. Explicit rearm still ignores previously consumed restock epochs. Pause cannot undo a tab already opened. Temporary installation disappears on Firefox restart.

The receiver key cannot pause the hosted monitor, access logs, or send Telegram messages. It permits receiving public-stock signals and occupying a vacant receiver slot, so keep the pairing file private. No card, password, cookies, checkout sessions, or incoming URLs are read.
