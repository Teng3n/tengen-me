Current geometric result: the aimed RLS6 main beams clear the measured fan blade plane. L1 is set to 27° and L2–L4 are set to 18°, all clocked toward the Window wall.

This is a geometric screening study using the field-measured blade plane, not a full photometric or time-domain flicker simulation. Published beam and field angles are intensity boundaries rather than hard cutoffs.

Calculated ceiling and fan values

  • ceilingHeight(x) = 148 − ((148 − 97.4) × x / 132)
  • Ceiling height at fan center X = 66″: 122.70″.
  • Field-measured fan blade plane: 95.60″ AFF.
  • Confirmed ceiling-to-blade distance at the fan center: 27.10″.
  • Ceiling change: 50.6″ over 132″; calculated angle: 20.97°.

Fixture and beam notes

  • Selected fixture: HALO RLS6159FS1EWHDMR; approximately 1,630 lumens per fixture, 45° beam, 70° field.
  • L1 uses the 27° pitch position; L2–L4 use 18°. All fixtures are clocked toward the Window wall.
  • coverage = angleBetween(fixtureAxis, fixtureToPoint) ≤ halfAngle
  • A1 samples the confirmed 95.60″ blade plane; A2 samples floor coverage and projects possible fan-shadow paths through that plane.
  • Beam and field angles are intensity boundaries, not hard edges or lux predictions.

Drawing conventions

  • Plan and elevation geometry use a one-inch coordinate basis.
  • Plan grid: 12″ major spacing with lighter 6″ subdivisions.
  • Coverage maps are sampled on a 2″ grid because aimed-beam footprints are not circular on a horizontal plane.
  • The projected side elevation collapses Y positions and must not be used for fan-overlap analysis.
  • Dimensions govern; do not scale from a print unless print scaling is 100%.

Remaining field variables

  • Monitor vertical placement and exact curved-screen surface position; the mounting plane is set flush to the Bathroom wall.
  • Final installed RLS6 aim, dimming level, surface reflectance, and real shadow contrast.
