Drawing conventions

  • Plan and elevation geometry use a one-inch coordinate basis.
  • Plan grid: 12″ major spacing with lighter 6″ subdivisions.
  • Beam circles and cones are clipped to the interior room boundary.
  • The projected side elevation collapses Y positions and must not be used for fan-overlap analysis.
  • Dimensions govern; do not scale from a print unless print scaling is 100%.
