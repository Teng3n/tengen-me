Measurement status summary

Confirmed measurements

  • Interior room: 144″ × 132″ (12′-0″ × 11′-0″).
  • Desk: 88″ Bathroom-wall run and 80″ Sideyard-wall run, edge-joined at X = 25″ with no overlap; 25″ depth.
  • Desktop: 29.5″ high, 1.5″ thick, 28″ underside.
  • Window: 72″ (6′-0″) wide, centered on Window wall; sill 41″ (3′-5″), head 81″ (6′-9″) AFF.
  • Ceiling endpoints: 144″ high and 96.5″ low.
  • Ceiling slopes from the Bathroom/high wall to the Window/low wall.
  • Fan: current 60″ Haiku L, centered at (72, 66), with a 22″ downrod.
  • Manufacturer overall fan height with that downrod: 29.1″ from ceiling to lowest point.
  • Lights: L1 (32,30), L2 (112,30), L3 (32,102), L4 (112,102).

Provisional measurements / assumptions

  • Exact fan blade plane is not published separately; the 29.1″ lowest-point plane is used as a conservative obstruction screen.
  • The prior drawing’s 22″ blade plane is retained only as an old-geometry comparison.
  • Monitor: 57″ Odyssey Neo G9, 52.3″ × 16.9″ × 13.3″ maximum depth, 1000R curve; centerline 44″ from Sideyard wall.
  • Monitor bottom is 5″ above the desktop.
  • Wafer-light scenario uses HALO HLBSL609FS5 photometrics and follows the ceiling’s 18.26° slope.

This is a conservative geometric screening study, not a full photometric or time-domain flicker simulation. The exact Haiku L blade plane remains the decisive field measurement.

Calculated ceiling and fan values

  • ceilingHeight(x) = 144 − ((144 − 96.5) × x / 144)
  • Ceiling height at fan center X = 72″: 120.25″.
  • Current manufacturer overall fan height with 22″ downrod: 29.1″.
  • Confirmed lowest point / conservative obstruction plane: 91.15″ AFF.
  • Prior 22″ assumed plane: 98.25″ AFF; this was not the complete fan height.
  • Ceiling change: 47.5″ over 144″; calculated angle: 18.26°.

Fixture and beam notes

  • Wafer scenario: HALO HLBSL609FS5; 900 nominal lumens, 112° beam, 160° field.
  • The flush wafer follows the ceiling normal, so its axis tilts 18.26° toward the Bathroom/high wall.
  • coverage = angleBetween(fixtureAxis, fixtureToPoint) ≤ halfAngle
  • A1 samples the conservative 91.15″ screening plane; A2 samples floor coverage and projects possible fan-shadow paths.
  • The original HALO RLS6 is retained in Section D as a 45° / 70° vertically aimed baseline.
  • Beam and field angles are intensity boundaries, not hard edges or lux predictions.

Drawing conventions

  • Plan and elevation geometry use a one-inch coordinate basis.
  • Plan grid: 12″ major spacing with lighter 6″ subdivisions.
  • Coverage maps are sampled on a 2″ grid because tilted wafer footprints are not circular on a horizontal plane.
  • The projected side elevation collapses Y positions and must not be used for fan-overlap analysis.
  • Dimensions govern; do not scale from a print unless print scaling is 100%.

Provisional assumptions to field-verify

  • Exact blade plane within the 29.1″ fan assembly height.
  • Monitor wall offset, vertical placement, and exact curved-screen surface position.
  • Installed wafer orientation, dimming level, surface reflectance, and real shadow contrast.
